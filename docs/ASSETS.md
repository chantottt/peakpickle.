# PeakPickle assets

`client/public/peakpickle-logo.png` is the exact user-supplied paddle and ball logo. `docs/wireframe.png` is the user-supplied primary visual reference.

`client/public/pickleball-courts.jpg` is a locally saved court image by Brian Zajac from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/outdoor-pickleball-courts-surrounded-by-trees-and-grass-cNuo2I6bznQ), available under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The source describes it as a park-court rendering; it is a presentation placeholder, not a photograph of the fictional Philippine venues in the demo.

Player avatars display initials and deterministic colors rather than implying stock-photo identities.
